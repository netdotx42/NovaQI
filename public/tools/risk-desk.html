Plan a cash position

Use prices, costs and equity in the same account currency. Supports long cash-equity or linear spot positions only. No FX lots, futures, options, leverage or short positions. Defaults are hypothetical examples.

How the estimate works

Budget = equity × risk %. Size is rounded down to your quantity increment and constrained by both the stop-case budget and your cash allocation. Fixed fees are reserved in both constraints. Stop-case allowance = entry − stop + adverse slippage per unit. Net target estimate also subtracts the slippage allowance and fixed fees.

A gap, unexpected fees or poor liquidity can exceed the inputs. No live prices or currency conversion are supplied. Verify the instrument’s unit conventions and broker requirements independently.
