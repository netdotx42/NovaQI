Supply a chronological sequence

Paste one net R result per observation, oldest first. Separate numbers with commas, spaces or line breaks. Use a decimal point. No dates, currency signs or headers. Maximum 10,000 observations. This tool analyses supplied outcomes; it does not backtest market prices.

What the report means

Development includes the first N observations; evaluation includes all remaining observations. Each segment begins its cumulative curve at zero. No test of independence, statistical significance, robustness or tradability is supplied.

Profit factor is positive R divided by absolute negative R. It is undefined when there are no negative results. Flat outcomes count in the win-rate denominator and break a losing streak. Extra costs are subtracted from every supplied net R outcome, with no compounding or currency reconstruction.

Nothing is saved automatically. Export the report to keep the inputs and calculations. The graph shows only the full stressed sequence. Hypothetical examples are invented and do not represent actual performance.
