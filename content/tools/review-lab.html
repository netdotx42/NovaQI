Read the metrics correctly

Net R = net P/L ÷ initial planned risk. Win rate counts positive net results; zero results remain in the denominator. Average R includes every entry. Drawdown is the largest decline from a previous peak in cumulative R, starting at zero. It is not an equity drawdown percentage. Entries are ordered by close date, with insertion order breaking same-day ties.

Different sizing, currencies or rule versions can make comparisons misleading. This tool does not verify broker records, import trades automatically or establish a strategy’s edge. Store one account currency per ledger. No open positions or deposits are tracked.
