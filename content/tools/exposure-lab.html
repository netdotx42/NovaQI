Interpretation

Gross totals include cash. Hypothetical net liquidation applies the entered fee to each asset in both current and stressed scenarios; cash is unchanged. Net change compares these net totals. Asset concentration uses current gross total including cash as its denominator.

No probabilities, correlation estimates, slippage, taxes, withdrawal delays, leverage or derivatives are modelled. No order or wallet connection exists. Inputs do not autosave; export snapshots. Fees alone cannot represent the full difficulty of liquidation.
